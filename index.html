Tentang Kami Who we are

Profil About us

Pribumi Baraqah Sdn. Bhd. (1322733-T) adalah syarikat kontraktor pembinaan, penyelenggaraan dan perkhidmatan berdaftar dengan Kementerian Kewangan Malaysia (MOF: K10085610793592160). Pribumi Baraqah Sdn. Bhd. (1322733-T) is a reputable contractor in construction, facility maintenance, and procurement services registered with the Ministry of Finance Malaysia (MOF: K10085610793592160).

Beroperasi di 10-2, Binjai 8, Lrg Binjai, Kuala Lumpur, 50450 Kuala Lumpur di bawah kepimpinan Pengarah Encik Adryan Wong Hua De. Kami menyediakan perkhidmatan pembinaan awam, pengubahsuaian komersial, penyelenggaraan hawa dingin, reka bentuk hiasan dalaman, dan perolehan kerajaan dengan komitmen kualiti dan integriti yang kukuh. Operating from 10-2, Binjai 8, Lrg Binjai, Kuala Lumpur, 50450 Kuala Lumpur under the leadership of Director Encik Adryan Wong Hua De. We deliver civil construction, commercial fit-outs, air conditioning maintenance, interior decoration, and government procurement services with a steadfast commitment to quality and integrity.
